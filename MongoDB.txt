You open Mongo Express and your request just starts flowing,NodePort picks it up, kube-proxy routes it, and the Service passes it to the right pod.
Mongo Express then pulls the database address from the ConfigMap, connects to MongoDB through its Service, and logs in using the Secret(where sensitive data is stored e.g tokens,keys or passwords).
Everything just clicks and moves smoothly and seamlessly...more like a package passing through the right hands and with no delay till it gets exactly where it should.
